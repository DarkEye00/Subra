How credits work

A deck costs credits by its size, and you see the price before polishing. Credits are taken only when you download, so a deck we can't polish costs nothing. Unused credits carry over while the plan renews, up to {{ carryover }} months' worth.

Cards, renewals and cancelling

Paystack charges your card in KES each month and emails a receipt; a USD card is converted by your bank. If a payment fails, your credits keep working for {{ grace_days }} days while you fix the card. Cancel any time: there's no further charge, and credits last until the end of the month you paid for. M-Pesa can't be charged monthly, so M-Pesa customers pay per deck.
