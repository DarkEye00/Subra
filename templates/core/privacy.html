How we handle your files

SUBRA redesigns PowerPoint decks. This page explains what we store, why, for how long, and who else processes it. We act as a data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we store

  • Your account: email address and a hashed password.
  • Your decks: the file you upload, the polished file, and preview images of its slides.
  • Payments: the M-Pesa number you pay from, the amount, the M-Pesa receipt number, and the status messages our payment provider sends us.
  • Service records: order history and technical logs, such as processing times and costs.
    • {% if subscriptions %}
  • Plans and company accounts: your company's name, the email addresses of its members and of people invited, who used how many credits on which deck, and, for card subscriptions, the last four digits, brand and expiry month of the card and Paystack's reference for it. Paystack holds the card itself; we never see or store the card number.
    • {% endif %}

How long we keep it

Unpaid decks and their previews are deleted automatically {{ unpaid_hours }} hours after they're ready, or after upload if you never polish them. Paid decks are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after payment. You can also discard an unpaid deck yourself at any time. Deletion is permanent: we can't recover a deck after that.

We keep order and payment records (without the files) for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax, and your account until you ask us to close it.

Who can see your deck

{% if subscriptions %}If you're a member of a company account, the company's owner can see and download the decks you upload while you're a member, and how many credits you used. Other members can't. {% endif %}Nobody at SUBRA opens your deck unless you ask us for help with an order. Files are stored in a private storage bucket, and download links expire after one hour.

Other companies involved

  • Anthropic (Claude API): to plan the new layout, we send the text and structure of your slides, such as paragraphs, table sizes and chart names, to Anthropic's Claude API. We don't send your images. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms.
  • Paystack and Safaricom (M-Pesa): process your payment. Paystack receives your phone number, your email address and the amount.{% if subscriptions %} For card subscriptions, Paystack stores your card and charges it each month.{% endif %}
  • DigitalOcean: hosts our servers and file storage.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and any files we still hold. Email {{ support_email }}. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
